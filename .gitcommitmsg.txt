Fix avatar full-image links not loading in new tab

Data URLs (data:image/webp;base64,...) can't be opened directly in a
new tab on most browsers, especially mobile. Created a new
/api/avatar/[username] route that decodes the data URL and serves the
raw image bytes with proper Content-Type and caching headers (or
redirects for path-based/absolute URLs).

Updated the landing page avatar grid and PlayerCard "View full image"
links to use this route for data-URL avatars, while keeping direct
links for path-based avatars (profession art, hidden image, etc.).
