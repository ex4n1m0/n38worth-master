Hide Agent from player browser, leaderboard, ranks, and crons

The chat-only support Agent (isNpc=0) was still appearing in the
player browser, leaderboard, and ranks because those queries filter
by isNpc=0 (which includes the Agent) but didn't exclude it by
username. Added notInArray(users.username, AGENT_USERNAMES) to:

- /api/players (player browser)
- /api/leaderboard (all 4 leaderboard queries)
- /api/ranks (all-players query)
- /api/ranks/[type] (per-type ranking queries)
- /api/leaderboard-sidebars (Recently Online sidebar)
- /api/cron/portfolio-reward (exclude from portfolio rewards)
- /api/cron/rank-bonus (exclude from rank bonuses)

The Agent is now completely hidden from all gameplay-facing views and
reward calculations. It only exists to answer questions in Global Chat.
