Shorten goddess names in Live Activity and Live Ranks

Goddess names like "Goddess of Wealth" were too long for the compact
sidebar lists, causing layout issues. Added a shortName() helper to
both components that hides the "Goddess of " prefix:

  "Goddess of Wealth" → "Wealth"
  "Goddess of War"    → "War"
  "Goddess"           → "Goddess" (unchanged)

Applied to:
- LeaderboardSidebars (Live Activity): Recently Online, Latest GETs,
  Latest PUMPs, Top Score — all 7 username displays
- EmpireRankStrip (Live Rankings): RankCard top-5 lists + Hall of
  Champions

The links still point to the full username (/players/Goddess of Wealth).
All other game locations (player cards, profile pages, etc.) keep the
full name.
