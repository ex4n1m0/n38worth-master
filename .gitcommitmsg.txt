Auto-claim daily reward when new player reaches 20 cards

New players often don't know to visit Home to claim their daily login
reward after unlocking the game at 20 cards. Now the reward is auto-
claimed server-side the moment mission 0 completes (missionLevel
advances from 0 → 1), regardless of whether the player navigates to
Home.

- Extracted the claim logic from the /api/game/login-reward route into
  a shared src/lib/login-reward.ts module (claimLoginReward).
- Refactored the route to use the shared function.
- Called claimLoginReward(buyerId) inside checkAndAwardMissions when
  currentLevel reaches 1 (mission 0 just completed).
- Safe for AI players: no-op if already claimed within 24h, and AI
  players won't be the richest human so they still get a reward.
